I am an M.S. candidate at KAIST Graduate School of Mobility, advised by Prof. Dong Soo Har.

My research focuses on reliable and safe learning systems for embodied AI, particularly vision-language-action models, uncertainty estimation, failure detection, and robust decision-making under distribution shift.

I am interested in understanding when and why embodied policies fail, and in using uncertainty signals to enable safer and more reliable deployment in real-world robotic systems.
