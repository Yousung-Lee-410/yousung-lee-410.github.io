About

I am interested in reliable and safe learning systems for embodied AI. My current work focuses on vision-language-action models, uncertainty estimation, failure detection, and robust decision-making under distribution shift.

I am particularly interested in understanding when and why embodied policies fail, how uncertainty can be used to detect such failures, and how these signals can support safer and more reliable deployment in real-world robotic systems.
